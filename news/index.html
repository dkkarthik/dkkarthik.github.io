2026

[Sep 26] DRONES Lab goes to IROS. We had 1 workshop invited talk, 2 conference papers, and 12 workshop papers across 7 workshops at IROS 2026. Our work received 2 workshop awards: the Best Paper Runner-Up Award at the Bridge4Nav workshop for “Learning When to Jump for Off-road Navigation,” and Best Student Poster at the Data in Field Robotics workshop for “CLPV: Dataset of Controlled Co-Located Lighting and Photometric Variation for Robot Vision.” Congratulations to both teams!
Links: DRONES Lab at IROS — talks and papers · Bridge4Nav award certificate · Data in Field Robotics award announcement

[Sep 26] Our paper AnyNav: Visual Neuro-Symbolic Friction Learning for Off-road Navigation has been accepted to The International Journal of Robotics Research (IJRR). Congratulations to Taimeng Fu and the SAIR Lab team!
Links: Project webpage · Paper · Publication entry

[Aug 26] Our recycled-plastics sensing technology co-led by Charuvahan Adhivarahan received a 2026 R&D 100 Award and R&D 100 Special Recognition in Corporate Social Responsibility (Bronze). Developed with our UB collaborators, the technology combines sensing techniques and machine learning to measure recycled plastic content in commercial products.
