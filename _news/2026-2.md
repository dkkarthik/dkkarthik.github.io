**[Feb 26]** Our article “Comparative Analysis of Feature Tracking Challenges in Unstructured Space Environments” has been published in IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems (TAES). The study evaluates ten handcrafted and learning-based visual features using real space imagery from surface, aerial, and orbital viewpoints, alongside Earth-based benchmarks. It examines how changing illumination, low-texture surfaces, and repetitive terrain make features difficult to track, offering insights toward more reliable visual navigation for future spacecraft. Congratulations to [TJ Chase](https://tjchase34.github.io/) for leading this work, with co-author Andrew Balotin!<br>
