**[Nov 26]** Our team received the **R&D 100 Awards** at the [R&D Awards Gala Banquet](https://www.rd100awards.com/) in Scottsdale, AZ on Nov 19th. The work, previously published in [Nature Communications Engineering]({{ "/publications/" | relative_url }}#recycled), demonstrated multiple sensing methods to identify post-consumer recyclable plastics and employed machine learning for detection as well as fusion. This work was one of the R&D 100 Award winners and also received the R&D 100 Special Recognition in Corporate Social Responsibility (Bronze).
