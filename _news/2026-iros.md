**[Sep 26] DRONES Lab goes to IROS.** We had **1 workshop invited talk, 2 conference papers, and 12 workshop papers across 7 workshops** at IROS 2026. Our work received **2 workshop awards**: the **Best Paper Runner-Up Award** at the [Bridge4Nav workshop](https://bridging-navigation.github.io/website/) for “Learning When to Jump for Off-road Navigation,” and **Best Student Poster** at the [Data in Field Robotics workshop](https://jonasfrey96.github.io/data_in_field_robotics/) for “CLPV: Dataset of Controlled Co-Located Lighting and Photometric Variation for Robot Vision.” Congratulations to both teams!<br>
