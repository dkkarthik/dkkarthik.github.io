I am an Associate Professor in Computer Science and Engineering at the University at Buffalo. I direct the <a href="https://drones.cse.buffalo.edu" target="_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener">Distributed RObotics and Networked Embedded Sensing (DRONES) Lab</a>, serve as the Founding Director of the <a href="https://www.buffalo.edu/cear.html" target="_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener">Center for Embodied Autonomy and Robotics</a>, and co-direct the <a href="https://engineering.buffalo.edu/mechanical-aerospace/graduate/graduate-programs/engineering-sciences-robotics.html" target="_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener">Master's in Robotics</a> program.

My interests are broadly in robot perception including task-specific representations and efficient perception for real-world operation. Most recently, my group has been working on two real-world applications with an emphasis on safe autonomy: <a href="https://droneslab.github.io/EARTH/" target="_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener">excavation autonomy</a> and <a href="https://droneslab.github.io/INSPECTOR/" target="_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener">culvert inspection</a>.
